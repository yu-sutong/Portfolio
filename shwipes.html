Shwipes

TikTok TechJam 2024 finalist project

Shwipes is a personalized shopping concept for TikTok Shop that applies machine learning and computer vision to help users browse products through swipeable cards.

Summary

The project explored how short-form shopping discovery could be made more interactive through swipe-based recommendations and machine learning.

Recognition

Shwipes was a top 15 finalist in Southeast Asia at TikTok TechJam 2024.
