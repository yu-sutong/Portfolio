ShopSpot

AI vision shopping browser extension

ShopSpot is a Chrome extension that turns YouTube videos into shoppable experiences using computer vision and recommendation systems. It captures video frames, identifies products, and surfaces swipeable shopping cards directly within the viewing flow.

Summary

The extension combines browser tooling, AI vision, and product search to connect content viewing with commerce discovery. It explores how shopping signals can be surfaced more naturally while people watch videos.

Notability

ShopSpot was submitted to HackNC 2025.

Technology

  • Platform: Chrome extension (Manifest V3)
  • Vision: browser capture and multimodal AI tooling
  • Commerce and recommendation: product search and preference-based suggestions
